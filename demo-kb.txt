Password Reset Policy

If a customer does not receive a password reset email:
1. Ask them to check spam, promotions, and blocked sender folders.
2. Confirm the email address on the account.
3. Ask them to wait 10 minutes before requesting another reset.
4. If the issue continues, escalate to engineering to check email delivery logs.

Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of purchase if the service was not used heavily.
Refund requests should include the purchase date, account email, and reason for cancellation.

Billing Issue Policy

If a customer reports a duplicate charge:
1. Confirm the transaction date and amount.
2. Check whether both charges belong to the same billing period.
3. If duplicate billing is confirmed, escalate to billing operations for reversal.
4. Apologize and provide a timeline for resolution.
